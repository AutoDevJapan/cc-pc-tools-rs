# Album video preparation

Keep the owner's audio and lyric sources unchanged. Store private source
receipts, recordings, lyric text, drafts, and upload records outside Git.
An intermediate preparation checkpoint is not a completed or published album.

## Release scope

The current album has fourteen tracks. The owner selected one complete album
video with chapters and fourteen individual track videos. Keep the exact
question, current owner reply, source version, and original task together in
the private operation records. This choice establishes the release units;
the visual direction still needs its own concrete image review.

## Source checks

- Record each source file's original name, title, byte size, and SHA-256.
- Read titles and credits from the supplied documents. Retain folder and file
  aliases when they differ; suffixes such as copy or edit are source metadata.
- Probe duration, sample rate, channels, and codec, then decode every full file.
- Measure loudness and true peak without modifying the source. Check the final
  encoded audio separately before deciding whether a correction is necessary.
- Preserve the full supplied performances, including short tracks, and record
  the track order from the owner's supplied material. Derive chapter boundaries
  from cumulative decoded audio sample counts with an explicit sample-rate
  timebase, then verify them against the final encoded video timeline.

## Visual review and production

Create concrete image proposals, then record the owner's explicit answer for
those exact images and scope before editing the video.
Keep the original image bytes, producer, input source, question body, decision
scope, and expected hashes together. Verify the actual Discord attachments
and question text before treating a request as delivered. Record visual approval
only from the owner's verified answer to that question; a failed send is
neither delivery nor approval.

Apply the current anonymity rules throughout production. Use abstract imagery,
text and lyrics for this album; do not invent or expose a member's identity,
appearance, animal counterpart or character. Keep the previously supplied
recordings and do not create new songs for the videos. Confirm audible playback
by measurement and listening, rather than accepting a silent audio stream.

If an attachment prerequisite is unavailable, retain the same request and
images, report the technical condition without an owner notification, and
repair the normal delivery path. Continue independent audio, lyric, metadata,
and channel checks. Keep protection records and original permissions intact;
do not bypass a rejected operation or disable the general sending protections.
If a later owner instruction supersedes an unsent proposal, retain its history
and prepare a compliant new proposal with its own explicit decision scope.

## Completion checks

Verify all fifteen complete outputs, lyrics and credits, visual consistency,
frame boundaries, final audio, and channel identity. Resolve independent
review findings against the exact final artifacts. Before uploading, verify a
release manifest that links the approved image hashes and owner answer to the
actual source assets, final video hash and review results. An edited image or
changed visual scope requires a new matching approval; source images and
encoded videos naturally have different hashes. Confirm each actual upload
and public result, retain the release ledger, and finish temporary-file and
owned-browser-tab cleanup. Preparation, owner direction, rendering, review,
upload, and public availability remain separate recorded stages.
